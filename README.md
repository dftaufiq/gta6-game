**SOLENA BAY** — an open-world crime game at golden hour, built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make GTA 6" bounty. Explore a sun-soaked coastal city on foot and by car: steal cars, outrun the 5-star Solena PD (pursuit units, roadblocks with spike strips, the SWAT van, and the CONDOR-1 helicopter), run 10 story missions for Mama Luz — from a first car theft to a bank vault job and a final showdown at Pier 9 — fight Rico Vane's crew with 5 weapons picked from a radial weapon wheel, win 3 checkpoint street races, tune your ride at Bo's garage (engine, armour, respray), call on your crew (Tess swaps plates, Bo repairs, Ivy spots every cop), and climb the respect ranks from Nobody to Bay Legend while each district warms to you. Traffic, pedestrians, a minimap HUD with live SPD dispatch chatter, procedural radio, and saves — all in plain static files that play offline in desktop Chrome.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — no build step, no network needed.
